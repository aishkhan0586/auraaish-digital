What is SEO and how does it work? SEO (search engine optimization) is the practice of improving a website so it ranks higher in Google. Search engines crawl pages, judge relevance and quality, then show the best matches for each search. Why is my website not showing on Google? Common reasons: the site is new, Google has not indexed it, a noindex tag or robots.txt rule blocks it, or the content has little authority next to competing pages. A free audit shows which one applies to you. How long does SEO take to work? Most sites see early movement in 2 to 3 months and stronger results after 6 months or more. Timing depends on competition, site age, content quality and technical health. How much does SEO cost? It depends on your market, competition and goals. Many small businesses pay a monthly fee, while larger international projects cost more. Ask for a free audit first so the price matches what your site really needs. How can I rank number 1 on Google? No one can guarantee a number 1 spot. The steps that help most are targeting the right keyword, matching search intent, writing the most useful page on the topic, fixing technical issues and earning quality backlinks. What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads? SEO earns free organic rankings that build slowly and last. Google Ads buys paid placement that appears instantly but stops when the budget stops. Is SEO still worth it with AI search? Yes. AI Overviews and chat assistants pull answers from pages that already rank well and are clearly structured, so strong SEO also supports AI visibility (GEO and AEO). What are backlinks and why do they matter? Backlinks are links from other websites to yours. Search engines treat links from trusted, relevant sites as votes of confidence, which helps a page rank higher. [Free Audit](#contact) [Home](#home) / FAQs FAQs # SEO Questions, *Answered Simply* The most searched SEO questions on Google, in plain English. What is SEO and how does it work? SEO (search engine optimization) is the practice of improving a website so it ranks higher in Google. Search engines crawl pages, judge relevance and quality, then show the best matches for each search. Why is my website not showing on Google? Common reasons: the site is new, Google has not indexed it, a noindex tag or robots.txt rule blocks it, or the content has little authority next to competing pages. A free audit shows which one applies to you. How long does SEO take to work? Most sites see early movement in 2 to 3 months and stronger results after 6 months or more. Timing depends on competition, site age, content quality and technical health. How much does SEO cost? It depends on your market, competition and goals. Many small businesses pay a monthly fee, while larger international projects cost more. Ask for a free audit first so the price matches what your site really needs. How can I rank number 1 on Google? No one can guarantee a number 1 spot. The steps that help most are targeting the right keyword, matching search intent, writing the most useful page on the topic, fixing technical issues and earning quality backlinks. What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads? SEO earns free organic rankings that build slowly and last. Google Ads buys paid placement that appears instantly but stops when the budget stops. Is SEO still worth it with AI search? Yes. AI Overviews and chat assistants pull answers from pages that already rank well and are clearly structured, so strong SEO also supports AI visibility (GEO and AEO). What are backlinks and why do they matter? Backlinks are links from other websites to yours. Search engines treat links from trusted, relevant sites as votes of confidence, which helps a page rank higher. What is the difference between on-page and off-page SEO? On-page SEO covers your own site: content, titles, headings and internal links. Off-page SEO covers outside signals, mainly backlinks, brand mentions and reputation. What is technical SEO? Technical SEO makes sure search engines can crawl, render and index a site. It covers speed, mobile friendliness, HTTPS, sitemaps, canonical tags and structured data. How do I do keyword research? Start with what your customers type, then check search volume, difficulty and intent in tools such as Google Search Console, Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs or SEMrush. Choose phrases you can realistically rank for. How do I get my website listed on Google? Add your site to Google Search Console, verify ownership, submit an XML sitemap and make sure pages are not blocked from indexing. New pages usually appear within days to a few weeks. What is local SEO and how do I show up near me? Local SEO helps a business appear in searches tied to a place, such as plumber near me. It relies on a complete Google Business Profile, reviews, local citations and consistent name, address and phone details. What are GEO and AEO? GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structure content so AI tools and answer boxes can quote it, using clear answers, entities and schema markup.
