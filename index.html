FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers to the SEO questions people search on Google every day.

What is SEO and how does it work?

SEO (search engine optimization) is the practice of improving a website so it ranks higher in Google. Search engines crawl pages, judge relevance and quality, then show the best matches for each search.

Why is my website not showing on Google?

Common reasons: the site is new, Google has not indexed it, a noindex tag or robots.txt rule blocks it, or the content has little authority next to competing pages. A free audit shows which one applies to you.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most sites see early movement in 2 to 3 months and stronger results after 6 months or more. Timing depends on competition, site age, content quality and technical health.

How much does SEO cost?

It depends on your market, competition and goals. Many small businesses pay a monthly fee, while larger international projects cost more. Ask for a free audit first so the price matches what your site really needs.

How can I rank number 1 on Google?

No one can guarantee a number 1 spot. The steps that help most are targeting the right keyword, matching search intent, writing the most useful page on the topic, fixing technical issues and earning quality backlinks.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

SEO earns free organic rankings that build slowly and last. Google Ads buys paid placement that appears instantly but stops when the budget stops.

Is SEO still worth it with AI search?

Yes. AI Overviews and chat assistants pull answers from pages that already rank well and are clearly structured, so strong SEO also supports AI visibility (GEO and AEO).

What are backlinks and why do they matter?

Backlinks are links from other websites to yours. Search engines treat links from trusted, relevant sites as votes of confidence, which helps a page rank higher.

How Much Does SEO Cost? Pricing Explained

Short answer: SEO prices vary widely by country, competition and scope. Most agencies charge a monthly retainer, a one-time project fee or an hourly rate. A free audit shows what you really need.

What affects the price of SEO?

  1. Competition for your keywords.
  2. Website size and how many pages need work.
  3. Starting point: technical problems and thin content cost more to fix.
  4. Scope: content, link building, local SEO and AI search add work.
  5. Your target countries and languages.

How do SEO agencies charge?

  • Monthly retainer: ongoing work and monthly reports.
  • Project fee: a one-time audit or technical cleanup.
  • Hourly consulting: advice on a specific problem.

How do you avoid overpaying?

  1. Ask for a free audit first.
  2. Ask exactly what is included each month.
  3. Be careful with anyone who guarantees number 1 rankings.
  4. Ask for reports from Google Search Console and GA4.

How to Get Backlinks to Your Website

How Long Does SEO Take to Work?

Short answer: Most websites see early movement in 2 to 3 months and stronger results after 6 months or more. New sites and competitive keywords take longer.

Why does SEO take time?

Google must crawl and index your changes, new content needs time to earn trust and links, and your competitors are improving at the same time.

What changes the timeline?

  • Site age: new sites start with little trust.
  • Competition: hard keywords take longer.
  • Content quality: helpful pages rank faster.
  • Technical health: errors can hold you back.
  • Backlinks: trusted links speed things up.

What is a typical timeline?

  1. Month 1: audit and technical fixes.
  2. Months 2 to 3: pages get indexed and first ranking gains appear.
  3. Months 4 to 6: more keywords reach page one and traffic grows steadily.

💡 Tip: Watch impressions in Search Console. They usually rise before clicks do.

Short answer: A technical SEO checklist makes sure search engines can crawl and index your site and visitors get a fast, secure page.

  2. Mobile friendly layout.
  3. Page speed and Core Web Vitals in PageSpeed Insights.

  7. No broken links or long redirect chains.
  8. Unique titles and meta descriptions.
  9. Structured data where it fits.
  10. Pages report checked for indexing errors.

How to Do Keyword Research (Step by Step)

Short answer: Keyword research means finding the words your customers type into Google, then choosing the ones you can realistically rank for.

How do you do keyword research?

  1. List topics your customers care about.
  2. Check Google autocomplete and People also ask for real questions.
  3. Look up search volume and difficulty in Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs or SEMrush.
  4. Check search intent. See what the top results show.
  5. Pick one main keyword per page, plus related phrases.
  6. Start with low-competition keywords that show buying intent.

What are the types of keywords?

  • Informational: what is SEO.
  • Commercial: best SEO tools.
  • Transactional: hire an SEO expert.
  • Navigational: a brand name.

💡 Tip: Longer phrases like SEO for small business are easier to win than one-word keywords.

Local SEO: How to Rank on Google Maps (Near Me Searches)

Short answer: Claim and complete your Google Business Profile, collect real reviews and keep your name, address and phone number the same everywhere online.

How do you rank in the local map pack?

  1. Claim and verify your Google Business Profile.
  2. Choose the right main category.
  3. Fill in services, opening hours, photos and description.
  4. Keep your name, address and phone identical on your website and directories.
  5. Ask happy customers for reviews and reply to them.
  6. Add your city and local keywords to key pages.
  7. List your business in trusted local directories.

What does Google look at?

Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. You cannot change distance, but you can improve relevance and prominence.

💡 Tip: Add new photos regularly. A complete profile earns more calls and direction requests.

SEO vs Google Ads: Which Is Better for Your Business?

Short answer: SEO earns free organic traffic slowly but lasts. Google Ads buys instant traffic that stops when the budget stops. Many businesses do best using both.

How to Improve Website Speed (Core Web Vitals)

Short answer: A fast website ranks and converts better. Aim for LCP under 2.5 seconds, INP under 200 milliseconds and CLS under 0.1.

How do you speed up your website?

  1. Compress images and use modern formats like WebP.
  2. Lazy-load images below the fold.
  3. Remove unused plugins and scripts.
  4. Enable caching and use a CDN.
  5. Minify CSS and JavaScript.
  6. Use reliable hosting.
  7. Test again in PageSpeed Insights.

What do the three metrics mean?

  • LCP: how fast the main content loads.
  • INP: how quickly the page responds to taps and clicks.
  • CLS: how much the layout jumps while loading.

💡 Tip: Start with images. They are the biggest files on most pages.

Why Is My Website Not Showing on Google? 7 Fixes

Short answer: Most often Google has not indexed the page yet, or something on your site is blocking it.

What are the 7 common reasons?

  1. The site is new. Add it to Google Search Console and submit a sitemap.
  2. The page is not indexed. Check it with the URL Inspection tool.
  3. A noindex tag is left on after launch.
  4. robots.txt blocks important pages.
  5. Thin or copied content gives Google no reason to rank you.
  6. No backlinks from other sites.
  7. A manual action (rare). Check the report in Search Console.

💡 Tip: Type site:yourdomain.com into Google to see which pages are indexed.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most sites see early movement in 2 to 3 months and stronger results after 6 months or more. Timing depends on competition, site age, content quality and technical health.

How much does SEO cost?

It depends on your market, competition and goals. Many small businesses pay a monthly fee, while larger international projects cost more. Ask for a free audit first so the price matches what your site really needs.

How can I rank number 1 on Google?

No one can guarantee a number 1 spot. The steps that help most are targeting the right keyword, matching search intent, writing the most useful page on the topic, fixing technical issues and earning quality backlinks.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

SEO earns free organic rankings that build slowly and last. Google Ads buys paid placement that appears instantly but stops when the budget stops.

Is SEO still worth it with AI search?

Yes. AI Overviews and chat assistants pull answers from pages that already rank well and are clearly structured, so strong SEO also supports AI visibility (GEO and AEO).

What are backlinks and why do they matter?

Backlinks are links from other websites to yours. Search engines treat links from trusted, relevant sites as votes of confidence, which helps a page rank higher.

What is the difference between on-page and off-page SEO?

On-page SEO covers your own site: content, titles, headings and internal links. Off-page SEO covers outside signals, mainly backlinks, brand mentions and reputation.

What is technical SEO?

Technical SEO makes sure search engines can crawl, render and index a site. It covers speed, mobile friendliness, HTTPS, sitemaps, canonical tags and structured data.

How do I do keyword research?

Start with what your customers type, then check search volume, difficulty and intent in tools such as Google Search Console, Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs or SEMrush. Choose phrases you can realistically rank for.

How do I get my website listed on Google?

Add your site to Google Search Console, verify ownership, submit an XML sitemap and make sure pages are not blocked from indexing. New pages usually appear within days to a few weeks.

What is local SEO and how do I show up near me?

Local SEO helps a business appear in searches tied to a place, such as plumber near me. It relies on a complete Google Business Profile, reviews, local citations and consistent name, address and phone details.

What are GEO and AEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structure content so AI tools and answer boxes can quote it, using clear answers, entities and schema markup.

Basics like writing helpful content and fixing titles are possible with free tools such as Google Search Console. Technical issues and link building usually need experience.
